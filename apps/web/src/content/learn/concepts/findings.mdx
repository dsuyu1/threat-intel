# Findings

A **finding** is a unit of agent output you can triage, filter, and query.
Findings are separate from the [knowledge graph](/learn/concepts/knowledge-graph): they’re time-oriented “what happened / what matters” items.

## What a finding typically contains

Depending on the agent, a finding may include:

- **Title + summary** (often AI-generated)
- **Agent** that produced it (CVE, threat feed, exploit, malware, …)
- **Severity** signals (e.g. severity, CVSS)
- **Exploitation likelihood** signals (e.g. EPSS)
- **Pointers** to entities (e.g. `cveId`, `mitreIds`)
- **Source URL/title** for traceability

## How findings relate to the graph

Findings can:

- Reference entities (a CVE, a technique id, an actor name)
- Add context that later becomes relationships in the knowledge graph (when evidence supports it)

In other words:

- **Findings** are “items to review”
- The **graph** is “the connected model of everything”

## Why you might see no findings

- No sources are enabled (so there’s nothing to ingest)
- Agents haven’t run yet
- Your project has no integrations (scanner feeds, RSS, STIX, etc.)
