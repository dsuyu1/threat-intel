# Playbooks

Playbooks are incident-response runbooks generated and managed inside the platform.
They’re optimized for “what do we do next?” during an incident.

## Lifecycle: draft → approved → archived

- **Draft**: generated (or edited) but not yet the authoritative version
- **Approved**: reviewed and ready to run during an incident
- **Archived**: kept for history but not actively used

## How AI generates playbooks (what it uses as input)

AI-generated drafts can use context like:

- The actor / scenario context (when created from Navigator)
- Relevant techniques (TTPs)
- Your stack and integrations
- A triggering finding (when created from a specific finding)

## Post-incident review (PIR)

After an incident, you can run a post-incident review to:

- Record what happened
- Measure time-to-detect/respond/contain
- Capture what worked vs what didn’t
- Feed improvements back into detections and controls

## Playbooks vs detections (different tools)

- **Detections**: “How do we catch this technique in telemetry?”
- **Playbooks**: “When it happens, what steps do we take to respond?”
