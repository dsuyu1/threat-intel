# Projects & scoping

A **project** is the primary boundary for “what data and configuration belongs together”.
When you switch projects, you’re changing the scope for:

- Connected sources/integrations
- Agent runs and their outputs (findings, documents, graph updates)
- Dashboards, risk, and operational views

## Why projects exist

Threat intel is only useful when it’s scoped:

- Different business units care about different tech stacks and assets.
- One team’s alerting rules shouldn’t pollute another team’s view.
- Integrations (scanner feeds, TAXII connections, etc.) are often environment-specific.

Projects give you separation without needing multiple accounts or deployments.

## Practical implication

If you’re “missing data”, confirm you’re in the right project before troubleshooting ingestion.
