# Risk

The risk register is a lightweight way to track which scenarios matter, who owns them, and what “acceptable” looks like.

## Risk entries (what they are)

A **risk entry** is typically tied to a Navigator scenario and includes:

- Status (open / accepted / mitigating / closed)
- Category (operational / financial / compliance / reputational)
- Owner + notes
- Optional FAIR-lite estimates (frequency × loss magnitude)

## How risk is calculated

Risk is a combination of:

- Scenario priority (Navigator score)
- Your current posture (coverage, actions, detections)
- Your tolerance thresholds (see below)

## Risk tolerance profiles

A tolerance profile sets thresholds per category so “high risk” is consistent:

- Operational threshold
- Financial threshold
- Compliance threshold
- Reputational threshold

These thresholds affect which entries show as “over tolerance” and drive dashboards.

## Compliance mappings (relationship to risk)

Compliance mappings connect controls to scenarios/techniques/mitigations.
This lets you:

- Show audit evidence (“this control is covered by these detections/actions”)
- Identify gaps (“these high-risk scenarios have no mapped controls”)

## Relationship to Navigator

Navigator answers “what should we do next?”  
Risk answers “is this acceptable, and who owns it?”
