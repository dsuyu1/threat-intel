# Create a playbook

Playbooks are incident response runbooks.

Open Playbooks at [`/playbooks`](/playbooks).

## 1) Pick an incident type

Choose the incident type that matches what you’re planning for (e.g., ransomware, credential compromise).

## 2) Review the AI draft

When you generate a draft, review for:

- Assumptions about your stack
- Required roles and approvals
- Tooling steps (EDR, SIEM, IAM, backups, comms)

## 3) Approve the playbook

Once approved, it becomes the version your team should follow during a real incident.

## 4) Run a post-incident review (when applicable)

After an incident, run the review workflow to capture timings and improvements.
